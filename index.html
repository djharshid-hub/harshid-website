The Artist

Canada's Leading
Indian Crossover DJ

DJ Harshid is a driving force behind the new wave of global club sound — building a career on sets that don't fit neatly into any one box. Bollywood. Punjabi. Techno. Hip-Hop. Bass. He takes all of it and turns it into something the room has never heard before.

With over 6 million global streams and a Canada Tour that took him across Toronto, Vancouver, and Calgary — performing at the country's most respected venues — Harshid has built a reputation not just as a DJ, but as a cultural architect of the dancefloor.
